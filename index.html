About

Visual communication and digital media design across embodied media, information design, and interactive visual interfaces.

Ryo (Yilong Huang) is a visual communication and digital media designer whose practice explores how visual systems can move beyond static graphic expression and become a medium for sensing, interpreting, and communicating bodily, emotional, and environmental states.

With a practice background in visual communication and digital media, his work combines research-driven design, interactive prototyping, physiological data visualization, and media system thinking. His current portfolio focuses on care-oriented interaction, VR-based training, multimodal signal visualization, and wearable biofeedback interfaces.

Design Positioning

My work investigates how media design can translate invisible states into perceivable experiences. I am especially interested in how physiological data, AI-supported interpretation, immersive environments, and visual systems can support human perception, reflection, and decision-making. Rather than treating design only as visual output, I approach it as a research method for building relationships between body, data, interface, and context.
